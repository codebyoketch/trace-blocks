Overview

TraceBlocks is committed to protecting your privacy. This Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used when you access our supply chain transparency platform.

We operate under the laws of Kenya and aim to handle your personal information with care, transparency, and respect.

TraceBlocks does not sell your personal data. We never have and we never will.
Data We Collect

Account information — when you register, we collect:

Name (first, middle, last)
Username
Email address
Phone number
Account type (individual / organisation)
Organisation name & registration number
Password (stored as a secure hash)
Date of account creation

Supply chain event data — when you log events, we record the information you provide including goods details, locations, parties, logistics, and transaction metadata.

Technical data — we may automatically collect IP addresses, browser type, and page interaction data for security and performance purposes.

How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Create and manage your TraceBlocks account
  • Record and display supply chain events on-platform and on the VeChainThor blockchain
  • Authenticate your identity and protect your account
  • Send important service-related communications (not marketing)
  • Maintain platform security and prevent fraud
  • Improve platform performance and user experience

We do not use your data for profiling, behavioural advertising, or automated decision-making.

Blockchain & Public Data

This is the most important section of this policy. Please read it carefully.

Supply chain events you submit are written to the VeChainThor public blockchain. This data is permanent, immutable, and publicly accessible to anyone via a blockchain explorer. TraceBlocks cannot remove or alter blockchain records under any circumstances.

The on-chain data includes goods details, location information, dispatcher and recipient names (as entered), dates, and transaction IDs. Do not submit sensitive personal information that you do not want to be permanently public.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • VeChain blockchain — supply chain events are written to the public VeChainThor network as part of the core service
  • Legal requirements — if required by Kenyan law, court order, or lawful authority
  • Safety — if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights or safety of any person
Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Passwords stored using secure one-way hashing
  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Access controls limiting who can view account data
  • Regular review of security practices

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data held by TraceBlocks:

Access
Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
Correction
Ask us to correct inaccurate off-chain personal data
Deletion
Delete your account and off-chain data at any time via Profile settings
Objection
Object to processing of your personal data in certain circumstances

Note: these rights apply to off-chain data only. Blockchain data cannot be deleted or altered by anyone — including TraceBlocks.

Cookies

TraceBlocks uses essential session cookies to keep you logged in and maintain form state. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but doing so will prevent you from staying logged in to the platform.

Children

TraceBlocks is not intended for use by persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that a child has registered, we will delete the account promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of TraceBlocks after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

For significant changes, we will notify registered users by email where possible.
