02Description of Service

TraceBlocks provides a platform for recording, tracking, and verifying supply chain events on the VeChainThor blockchain. The service enables users to:

  • Register products and goods within a traceable supply chain
  • Log supply chain events including dispatch, transit, and delivery
  • View immutable, on-chain records of supply chain history
  • Share transparent traceability data with stakeholders

TraceBlocks was developed as part of the Zone01 Kisumu Hackathon 2025. The service is provided as-is for transparency and traceability purposes.
